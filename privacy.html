Ilm Quest

Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

In short

Ilm Quest does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your game progress (points, XP, level, streak, badges, your score in each category and in the daily quiz, and which questions you have answered) and your settings, such as whether vibration is on. This is saved only on your device so you can pick up where you left off.

What the app does not do

There is no account or sign-up. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Reminders and sharing

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules it itself; nothing is sent anywhere. If you share a score, the image and text go only to the app you choose, and only when you choose to share.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Reset progress to clear your progress at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has saved.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that uses data, such as online leaderboards, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
